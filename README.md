# Customer Behaviour & Commercial Performance Analytics

**Turning Customer Engagement Data into Business Insights with Python, SQL, and Tableau**

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on customer behaviour, promotional offer engagement, reward redemption, and commercial performance. The project explores customer interactions and transaction patterns to identify engagement trends and support data-driven marketing and business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse customer behaviour and promotional offer engagement.
- Measure offer view rates and completion rates.
- Evaluate customer engagement across age groups and gender.
- Examine reward redemption patterns across customer segments.
- Track transactions and revenue-related performance indicators.
- Build an interactive Tableau dashboard to communicate actionable insights.

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Offer Type Distribution
- Compare the distribution of discount, BOGO, and informational offers.
- Examine how promotional offers are distributed across customers.
- Identify differences in offer exposure.

### 2. Offer Engagement and Completion
- Compare offer view rates and completion rates by offer type.
- Evaluate the gap between viewing and completing an offer.
- Identify opportunities to improve promotional effectiveness.

### 3. Customer Demographics
- Analyse engagement across age groups.
- Compare view and completion rates by demographic segment.
- Examine gender distribution within the customer base.

### 4. Reward Redemption
- Compare reward redemption patterns across customer groups.
- Examine differences between male and female customers.
- Evaluate redemption efficiency across the displayed reward ranges.

### 5. Commercial Performance
- Monitor transaction volume and revenue indicators.
- Explore customer engagement in relation to commercial outcomes.
- Identify segments that may warrant further business analysis.

## 💡 Dashboard Highlights

The provided Executive Overview dashboard shows:

- **76.0% overall view rate**, indicating that a substantial share of offers sent were viewed.
- **Offer-type differences:** BOGO and discount offers have different viewing and completion rates.
- **Age-group variation:** engagement and completion rates differ across customer age groups.
- **Gender distribution:** the dashboard displays 58% male and 42% female customers.
- **Reward redemption patterns:** redemption efficiency is compared across gender and reward ranges.
- **Revenue and transactions:** headline KPIs summarise commercial activity.

These are descriptive observations from the dashboard; further statistical testing would be needed to establish causation.

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** inspect, clean, and prepare customer, offer, and transaction data.
2. **Data Analysis:** explore customer behaviour, engagement, and commercial metrics using Python and SQL.
3. **KPI Development:** calculate metrics related to offer views, completions, transactions, and revenue.
4. **Visualization:** develop a Tableau dashboard to present key metrics and segment-level comparisons.
5. **Business Interpretation:** identify behavioural patterns and potential opportunities for improving promotional performance.
